# Cursed Castle 🏰

A cursed castle, three relics and five minutes to break the spell.

Cursed Castle is a first-person 3D game developed in C++ and Vulkan for the Computer Graphics course at the Politecnico di Milano, academic year 2025/2026. 
The project combines exploration, environmental interactions and real-time rendering in a low-poly fantasy setting.

## Gameplay 
An ancient pact has cast a curse upon the castle. Explore its chambers, listen to the clues given by the statues, and recover three relics: the Sacred Grimoire, the Holy Chalice and the Cursed Blade.

Find the key to unlock the blocked areas and return the relics to the altar before the five minutes run out. Once the curse is broken, night gradually gives way to day: make your way outside to complete your escape.

## Features
1) First-person exploration with collisions with the environment
2) Animated doors, collectable items, locks and interactions with statues
3) Time-limited objective, victory and defeat screens, and the option to restart
4) Torches with animated flames and flickering light.
5) Visual transition from night to day linked to the game’s progression.
